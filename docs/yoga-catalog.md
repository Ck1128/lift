# Yoga catalog

Catalog `yoga-1`, generator `yoga-gen-1.0`. 13 styles, three levels (beginner, intermediate, regular), props (mat, blocks, strap, bolster, blanket, chair, cushion, wall) and restrictions. Plans are deterministic and cached by input; a started session keeps a frozen copy, so later catalog changes never rewrite history.

Safety: hot styles exclude inversions, beginners and intermediates do not get the most demanding poses, cautions stay visible, and the app gives general wellness guidance only. Prenatal yoga is intentionally not included (it needs qualified guidance). Yoga has its own level and is never inferred from the strength level.
